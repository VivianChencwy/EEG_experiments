================================================================================
                          THEORETICAL INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. Dataset Intrinsic Properties Matter
   • Signal strength affects optimal strategy
   • Pattern complexity determines emphasis needs
   • Learning dynamics vary by task

2. Domain Adaptation Must Be Tailored
   • P3 (weak signal): Needs adaptive emphasis + unified BN
   • AVO (strong signal): Benefits from equal treatment
   • One approach does NOT fit all

3. Adaptive Weighting is Universal Requirement
   • Only component that NEVER fails
   • Critical for both weak and strong signal datasets
   • Must evolve during training (not fixed)

4. Normalization Strategy Depends on Signal Quality
   • Weak signals: Unified BN better (statistical stability)
   • Strong signals: Either BN works (less critical)
   • Split-BN can hurt when samples are limited

5. Cross-Dataset Performance vs Target Performance
   • MMD helps overall balance
   • Removing MMD helps target but hurts source
   • Choose based on priority
