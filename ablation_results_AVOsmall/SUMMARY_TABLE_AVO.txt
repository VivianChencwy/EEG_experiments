================================================================================
                              KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. EQUAL WEIGHTS ARE BEST FOR AVO (OPPOSITE OF P3!)
   • Equal weights (Ablation 1) = BEST AVO performance (+10.51% vs worst)
   • AVO benefits from simpler, balanced training approach
   • May be due to AVO's stronger/more consistent patterns

2. FIXED WEIGHTS CONSISTENTLY WORST (SAME AS P3)
   • Fixed weights (Ablation 2) = WORST for both AVO (-10.51%) and P3 (-3.75%)
   • Adaptive weight evolution is CRITICAL regardless of dataset
   • Confirmed universal principle across datasets

3. MMD HELPS AVO BUT HURTS P3
   • Without MMD (Ablation 3): 2nd best AVO, but P3 suffers
   • MMD provides cross-dataset balance
   • Important when using domain-specific weighting

4. UNIFIED BN PROVIDES BEST OVERALL BALANCE
   • No Split-BN (Ablation 4): 3rd for AVO, but BEST overall balance
   • P3 improves significantly (0.6143)
   • Most robust strategy across different scenarios

                        DATASET-SPECIFIC INSIGHTS
================================================================================

Why Does AVO Behave Differently from P3?

1. TASK NATURE:
   • AVO: Visual oddball (stronger, more consistent responses?)
   • P3: Cognitive target detection (more variable, complex patterns?)

2. LEARNING DYNAMICS:
   • AVO may have simpler patterns → benefits from equal weights
   • P3 may have complex patterns → needs active emphasis when small

3. DOMAIN DOMINANCE:
   • When AVO small: P3 (80 trials) naturally dominates
     → Equal weights HELP AVO compete effectively
   • When P3 small: AVO (80 trials) dominates even more
     → P3 needs ACTIVE emphasis to avoid being washed out

CONCLUSION: Dataset characteristics matter! No one-size-fits-all solution.
